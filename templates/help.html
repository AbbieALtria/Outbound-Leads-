How the system works

A quick guide to the whole flow: find businesses → clean them up → hand a dial-ready list to VICIdial → feed call results back in. Hover most buttons in the app for a tooltip too.

1 · Clients & Campaigns

A Client is the customer you generate leads for (name, contact, website, address). A Campaign is one engagement for that client — it bundles an Offer (the pitch/scoring) and the VICIdial campaign id for redials. Industry & location are optional defaults; you actually pick them at pull time.

2 · Pull leads (Dashboard)

Pick a campaign (or “quick one-off”), choose an industry (or “All industries”) and one or more locations (the Multiple locations box sweeps several cities in one pull), set a target, and Pull New Leads. Leads come from Google Maps via Outscraper — name, phone, address, website, reviews, rating.

3 · Verify phones

Verify phones checks each number’s line type (landline/mobile/VoIP) and marks dead numbers invalid so they’re excluded from the dial export. Costs Outscraper credits (1 per number).

4 · Enrich contacts (Apollo)

Enrich contacts finds the decision-maker (name + title) so agents get past the gatekeeper — this is free. Optional email (1 credit) and direct dial (8 credits) reveals are toggled in Settings. Start with direct-dial OFF.

{% endif %}

5 · Export to VICIdial

VICIdial CSV gives a dial-ready file in your loader’s format. DNC numbers, invalid phones, and closed businesses are automatically excluded.

6 · DNC & compliance

Upload your DNC / litigator lists on the DNC page; matching leads are suppressed from every export. Keep documented consent for any B2C calling, and remember Canadian lists (TA Networks) fall under CRTC, not the US DNC.

7 · Requeue (redials)

Each night the system reads VICIdial dispositions and rebuilds a regenerated list: not-reached numbers go back in the pool (capped), “not interested” is paused 90 days, do-not-call is removed. Download the batch and re-upload when you’re ready — nothing goes to VICIdial automatically.

8 · Users & limits

Admins create users and set per-agent lead caps (daily + total) and campaign access — perfect for capped test accounts. New users must set their own password at first login. Admins are unlimited.
