What the calls are telling the scoring

For each campaign, every scoring signal is compared on the leads that have actually been contacted: how often did the leads it fired on end up interested, booked, or not interested, versus the leads it did not fire on? Outcomes come from lead statuses set on the dashboard and from imported VICIdial dispositions.

Analysis only. Nothing on this page changes a rule or rescores a lead. Suggested points are a starting point for a human decision — to apply one, edit the offer's rules in Settings. A signal needs {{ min_sample }}+ contacted leads before it is rated.
