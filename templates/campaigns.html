Campaigns

A campaign is a client engagement: it bundles a client, an offer (the pitch/scoring), and the VICIdial campaign it dials under — so leads and redial cycles stay tied to the same client with full history. Industry & location are optional defaults here; you actually choose them (and can sweep several cities) on the Dashboard at pull time, so one campaign can cover many cities/industries over time.

Lead generation is deduped per campaign: re-running a pull (weekly or on demand) never repeats a business already in this campaign — it only adds new ones. The same business can still be a lead for a different client's campaign.
